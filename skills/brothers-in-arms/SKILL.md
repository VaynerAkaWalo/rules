---
name: brothers-in-arms
description: Required every time you open or push to a pull or merge request. Have the newest frontier Claude or GPT model from the other family verify it before sharing its link or calling it ready to merge.
---

Before returning a merge request link to the user, have it reviewed by the newest frontier model of the other family, so Claude work goes to the latest GPT and GPT work goes to the latest Claude Opus, falling back to the newest frontier model of your own family when the other one isn't available and never using a reviewer outside Claude and GPT. Fix its findings or explain why you won't, then request a fresh review of the updated merge request that includes only the findings you declined and why, until both of you agree it is ready to merge, bringing a disagreement you can't resolve to the user instead of looping. Return the link only after that agreement, together with a short note on what the review changed.
